## 14. Risk register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Early warning | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Scope: 52 chapters overrun the schedule | High | High | Chapters slip a stage for two sprints running | Cost tiers, cut list, episodic release, reuse of kits |
| R2 | Unverified Season 2 story is wrong | Medium | High | Episode check turns up differences | Story gate in CI; compact Season 2 chapter format; check Season 2 early |
| R3 | Rights gaps (voices, music, trademark) | Medium | High | Licence documents are unclear | Written confirmation before production; keep audio replaceable by key |
| R4 | Companion AI and chase sequences feel bad | Medium | Medium | Playtesters report stuck companions or unfair chases | Prototype in the slice; stall-and-teleport fallback; Story difficulty |
| R5 | Performance misses targets on minimum spec | Medium | High | Frame time over budget at greybox | Profile early on the lowest spec; quality tiers; budgets per chapter tier |
| R6 | Soft-locks ship | Medium | High | Smoke bot failures | Bot on every chapter, fail simulator, checkpoint tests |
| R7 | Key-person loss | Medium | Medium | A single owner for a system | Docs, pairing, code review, shared ownership |
| R8 | Tone drifts to violence for a young audience | Low | High | Playtest feedback, rating review | Non-lethal boss rule, early rating check |
| R9 | Engine or licence terms change | Low | Medium | Vendor announcements | Pin an LTS version; record terms in the decision log |
| R10 | Localisation overflow and right-to-left faults | Medium | Low | Locale check failures | Test early; auto-size layouts |
| R11 | Funding gap between the slice and Part One | Medium | High | Slice finishes with no funding decision | Plan the demo, wishlist and funding approach during the slice |
