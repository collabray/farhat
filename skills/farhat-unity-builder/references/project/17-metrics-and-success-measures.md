## 17. Metrics and success measures

| Metric | Target (planning) | Where measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slice completion | 70% reach chapter 5 | Playtests |
| Crash-free sessions | Above 99.5% | Diagnostics, opt-in |
| Soft-locks found by the bot | Zero open at release | CI |
| Chapters passing the story gate | 100% of included chapters | CI |
| Frame time on minimum spec | Within budget in every chapter | Profiling |
| Review sentiment (after launch) | Majority positive | Store |
| Wishlist growth (before launch) | Tracked weekly | Store |
