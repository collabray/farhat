## 2. Scope, goals and constraints

**Goals**

1. Deliver a faithful playable adaptation of all 52 episodes in order.

2. Prove the five pillars (faithful journey, light against darkness, a party of friends, impulse against wisdom, wonder for all ages) in a vertical slice before scaling.

3. Build a pipeline in which adding or correcting a chapter is a data change plus a level pass.

4. Ship family-safe: PEGI 7 / ESRB E10+ target, full accessibility options, no online features.

**Non-goals**

- Multiplayer, online services, microtransactions, open-world exploration.

- Branching story outcomes (the show's canon is fixed; choices change tone and rewards only).

**Success criteria (planning targets, adjust to the studio)**

| Area | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Vertical slice | Three chapters (1, 5, 7) playable end to end, internally rated fun by at least 80% of playtesters |
| Performance | 60 frames per second on the minimum PC spec, 30 on the lowest console spec |
| Stability | Crash-free sessions above 99.5% in beta |
| Completion | Players who start chapter 1 reach chapter 5 in at least 70% of playtests |
| Accessibility | All options in bible section 8 present and tested |

**Assumptions**

- The studio holds the licence for the game rights, music and voices (stated by the project owner; confirm in writing before production).

- The episode check against the licensed episodes happens before each chapter's content lock.

- The team will use stylised art and reusable level kits, not photoreal assets or motion-capture-heavy animation.
