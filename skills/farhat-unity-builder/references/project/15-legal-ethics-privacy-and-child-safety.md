## 15. Legal, ethics, privacy and child safety

- **Licence:** keep the signed licence, voice and music permissions in the repository's legal folder; check they cover games, all platforms, all territories and episodic or DLC distribution.

- **Trademark and names:** use the licensed names and designs only; the bible's glossary tracks the alternate dub names so the localisation team can choose the right set per territory.

- **Children's privacy:** the game is single-player with no accounts. Collect nothing by default. If analytics or crash reporting are added, make them opt-in, avoid personal data, and check the laws that apply to children's data in each target market with counsel.

- **Age rating:** complete the classification process for each store and region and re-check after each episode.

- **Content safety:** no gore, no real-world weapons beyond the show's fantasy sword, no online chat, no gambling-style mechanics, no manipulative monetisation.

- **Accessibility:** follow the options in bible section 8 and test them; treat them as release requirements, not extras.

- **Third-party assets:** log the licence of every purchased asset, plugin and font; keep proof of purchase and permitted use.

- **Credits:** credit the series' creators and producers as the licence requires, plus the full production team and tools.
