## 16. Product and business options

| Question | Options | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing | Premium single price; episode pricing; free demo plus paid episodes | Free or low-cost demo of the slice, then episode pricing |
| Monetisation | Premium only; cosmetic DLC | Premium only; no loot boxes or pressure mechanics for a young audience |
| Platforms | PC first; consoles later | PC first, consoles after the slice proves out |
| Marketing | Demo, wishlists, fan community, press, festivals | Start with the fan community of the series and a demo |
| Localisation | Original series languages first | Plan the languages in pre-production; the series is Italian in origin |
| Updates | Free updates, paid DLC, separate releases | Decide with the episode model in section 12.6 |
