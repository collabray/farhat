## 15. Production plan

**Scope at a glance [G]**

| Item | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Chapters | 52 |
| Distinct locations and hubs | About 20 |
| Boss and set-piece encounters | About 20 |
| Playable characters in segments | Farhat, Tilda, Wings, Shadow (plus brief Ben and Shulena) |
| Cutscene time | 3 to 5 minutes per chapter |
| Core systems | Combat, light meter, Shadow, alchemy, decode, vehicles, cover-up, disguise |

**Phases [G]**

1. **Pre-production:** episode verification pass over all [I] chapters, script lock for dialogue, art and audio bible, prototype of the core combat and Shadow.

2. **Vertical slice:** chapter 1 (exploration and tone), chapter 5 (Shadow, quicksand, parallel play) and chapter 7 (balloon chase, shadow contest). Proves every pillar.

3. **Alpha, Part One:** chapters 1 to 26 playable with greybox art in the later ones.

4. **Beta, Part Two:** chapters 27 to 52 playable, all systems feature-complete.

5. **Release candidate and gold:** polish, localisation, accessibility sign-off, ratings submission.

**Roles needed:** game director, narrative lead and two writers, lead designer, systems and level designers, character, environment and VFX artists, animators, composer and audio team, engineers (gameplay, AI, tools), QA lead, producer. Team size and schedule are set by the studio.

**Technical needs [G]:** third-person character controller with companion AI; a vehicle framework (balloon, flying transport, horse); a checkpoint and fail-forward system; a dialogue system with tone tags; a cutscene tool; localisation support; accessibility options listed in section 8.

**Quality gates.** (1) Story gate: every chapter checked against its episode before content lock. (2) Fail-state gate: every set piece has a fair checkpoint and no hard fail. (3) Audience gate: no gore, content reviewed for ages 8 and up. (4) Accessibility gate: all options in section 8 present and tested.

**Risks**

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Season 2 story is only partly verified | Wrong story beats | Mandatory episode check before Part Two content lock; the three-line format limits rework |
| Scale: 52 chapters | Schedule and budget overrun | Reuse hubs and systems; vertical slice first; make rest chapters cheap |
| Tone drift toward violence | Rating risk | Non-lethal boss rule and dissolve effects |
| Voice and music rights | Legal and cost | Settle with the rights holder before casting |
| Canon contradictions | Fan trust | Continuity table (section 3.5) maintained as a living page |
