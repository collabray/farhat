## 4. Characters

### Playable and party

**Farhat (playable, always).** Sixteen-year-old prince, brave, handsome, impulsive. *Arc:* from returning student to chosen hero, learning that rash action feeds Egokhan's traps. *Play:* sword combat, parry, light channelling, traversal. *Voice:* warm, quick, sometimes cocky.

**The Shadow (companion ability).** Farhat's playful, spiteful opposite: carefree, never serious, easily frightened, fiercely loyal. It can change form. *Arc:* captured in ch. 5, central to ch. 7, 23 and 25, origin revealed in Season 2. *Play:* scout, slip through gaps, swap positions, distract enemies, take shapes. *Voice:* teasing, comic.

**Wings (trainer, crafting hub, controlled in some segments).** Scorpion warrior and alchemist, formal old-fashioned speech, an expert in herbs. *Arc:* teacher, near-death in ch. 22, enslaved and monstrous in Season 2. *Play:* alchemy hub, training sections, heavy-hitter ally.

**Tilda Swanson (playable in puzzle chapters).** Fifteen-year-old Oxford genius in archaeology, languages, history and astronomy; speaks slang to spite Oxford English; honest, in love with Farhat without knowing it. *Arc:* leaves Oxford to follow the quest, rivalry with Shulena, then with Laylah. *Play:* map and code decoding, science gadgets, laptop scanning.

**Ben (hub NPC, support).** Farhat's butler since childhood, loyal, with cousins scattered across the world. *Arc:* betrayal for his cousins (ch. 20), amnesia (ch. 23), corruption (Season 2). *Play:* supplies, hints, save points, cousin-network side tasks.

**Ben's cousins (side network).** Include Ben Saburo from Japan, who gives Ben a deadly frying-pan katana. *Play:* optional gift and shortcut unlocks.

**Laylah (Season 2 party member, then antagonist).** Brilliant Siberian girl, in love with Farhat, rival of Tilda. Her alchemy skills turn dark under the Namirash. *Play:* brewing partner early, then a boss.

**Queen Shulena (Season 1 ally).** Mayan queen, knows secret jungle herbs, fascinated by Farhat. *Play:* temporary party member in ch. 14 and 15.

### Family and supporting

**King D'Emir Yassir and Queen Dolunai.** Farhat's parents. Dolunai urges him to begin the journey and her voice breaks the Rashid spell. They are captured, charmed into Guardians of the Labyrinth, and revived at the end of Season 1.

**Nigel Swanson.** Tilda's father, a miner in England, protective and kind. He seeks advice from the tree that holds his late wife's spirit.

**Greta Swanson.** Tilda's late mother, a spirit in a tree in Season 2 who can move objects.

**Miss (Constance) Keyhole.** Strict Oxford tutor sent to bring Tilda home. She disbelieves the quest until she sees Egokhan at Stonehenge, then helps; in Season 2 she focuses on Laylah.

**Professor Muff.** The old Oxford professor Farhat first seeks to read the map.

**Zenem (Aladdin).** The sage who drew the map; his spirit later helps restore Wings.

**Tabagee.** Listed as a supporting character; role not confirmed [I]. Reserve for a minor NPC and confirm in the episode check.

### Antagonists

**Egokhan.** Demon who removed the Shariman seven thousand years ago. Shapes himself into pyramids, labyrinths, robotic horsemen and elemental storms. *Arc:* adversary through both seasons. *Play:* final boss of Season 1 and recurring elemental boss.

**Rashid Al Wazum.** President of Petroglobe, an oilman who wants the Emirates. Uses machines: artificial-shadow device, mechanical bulldog, giant robot, scorching attacks, disguises. *Arc:* arrested at the end of Season 1, turned good in Season 2 and named Guardian of Ashtarak, later enslaved by Laylah. *Play:* comic-menacing recurring boss, later ally.

**Laylah (corrupted).** Season 2's human antagonist. See above.

**The Namirash.** The dark complement of the Shariman. Not a character but a force: reverse effects, black holes.
