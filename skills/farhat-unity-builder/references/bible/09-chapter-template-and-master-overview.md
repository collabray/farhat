## 9. Chapter template and master overview

Every chapter is one episode. Each entry uses the same five lines so a correction from the episode check touches one line only.

- **Story beats:** what happens in the episode, with its tag.

- **Level and objectives [G]:** where it is played and what the player must do.

- **Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** systems used and what opposes the player.

- **Fail states [G]:** what counts as failing, drawn from section 7.

- **Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** key scenes and what the chapter gives the player.
