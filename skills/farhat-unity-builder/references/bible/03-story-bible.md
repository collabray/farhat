## 3. Story bible

### 3.1 World logic

- **The Shariman** is a magic crystal. In the end it proves to be the heart and fuel source of an ancient machine that brings life to the desert with clean, everlasting energy. [V]

- **Egokhan** removed it seven thousand years ago and shattered it. It exists as **seven fragments** scattered around the world. [V]

- **Zenem** (a sage of long ago) drew the map that shows where the fragments lie. **Wings**, his eternalised scorpion, keeps it and delivers it to Farhat. [V]

- **Petroglobe** is Rashid's oil company. Its drilling in the forbidden part of the desert is what first disturbs the crystal's resting place. [V]

- The **scorpion constellation** changes in the sky as the prophecy comes true; Tilda reads this as the fulfilment of a foretold destiny. [V]

- **Season 2 adds the Namirash** (also spelled Namyrash), the dark complement of the Shariman. Its influence makes everything go backwards. Black holes open, and the Shariman must be used to close them. [V]

### 3.2 Season 1 arc: The Prince of the Desert

1. **Setup (ch. 1 to 6):** Farhat returns home, trains with Wings, receives Zenem's map, finds Tilda in England, loses and recovers his Shadow, and the first fragments are found. Egokhan and Rashid ally against him.

2. **First tour (ch. 7 to 13):** Cappadocia, the desert village, Miss Keyhole's visit, Rashid's disguises and Mexico. The first great test is losing his own identity as Rashid (ch. 11).

3. **The temple and the pyramid (ch. 14 to 17):** the Mayan valley, the Red Death, the pyramid that swallows him, and the telepathic call of his mother that tempts him into a trap.

4. **Trust and loss (ch. 18 to 24):** Stonehenge, the volcano plant, Ben's betrayal and memory loss, the duel for the sixth fragment, and Wings's ageing.

5. **Finale (ch. 25 to 26):** all seven fragments united, the mine rescue, the stolen crystal, and the maze where Farhat must face his own parents under Egokhan's spell.

### 3.3 Season 2 arc: The Black Scorpion

- **Opening [V/I]:** the Shariman powers the Emirates and the friends live happily. To understand where the crystals came from they plan a journey through the world of dreams to a place seven thousand years in the past (ch. 27).

- **New enemy [V]:** Farhat must now find the Namirash. Black holes open (one in Beijing's Olympic stadium, ch. 33). To close them the Shariman must be pulverised, and only a **Guardian of Ashtarak** may do it. Rashid, under the Namirash's reversing influence, turns good and is named Guardian.

- **Laylah [V]:** a brilliant Siberian girl who loves Farhat and starts as Tilda's friend. Corrupted by the Namirash (part of which lives inside her) she becomes jealous, nearly kills Tilda with Wings's potions turned to black magic, and wants to reunite the Namirash and make Farhat her consort. After her defeat she returns to being an ordinary Russian girl.

- **Corruption [V]:** Ben is corrupted into Ben in Black until the Beijing black hole is closed. Wings and Rashid become Laylah's will-less servants; Wings turns into a many-tentacled monster until the spirit of his master Zenem (Aladdin) restores him.

- **Revelations [V]:** Farhat was born without a shadow. The Shadow joined him on a night of lunar eclipse and partly comes from Namirash fragments that escaped the darkness (ch. 48, *Heart of Darkness*). Tilda's mother Greta, dead, lives on as a spirit in a tree and advises Nigel.

- **Endgame [I]:** the final chapters (*Great Manoeuvres*, *On the Edge of the Abyss*, *Farhat Against Everybody*, *The Lacking Grain*) resolve Laylah, the Namirash and the black holes. Exact order of events is confirmed during the episode check.

### 3.4 Themes and tone

Friendship and trust, tested by betrayal (Ben) and by manipulation (Laylah); responsibility against impulse; family as the source of strength (Dolunai's voice breaks the spell in ch. 11); light and darkness as complements rather than pure good and evil (Season 2). Tone: bright adventure, humour from the Shadow and Ben's cousins, real emotional stakes, no graphic violence.
