## 10. Season 1 chapters (Part One)

### Chapter 1: The Prince Comes Home

**Story beats [V]:** Farhat returns to the Emirates from Oxford and is joyfully reunited with Ben, his playful Shadow, his mother Dolunai and his father D'Emir Yassir. The King is dealing with Rashid, president of Petroglobe, who wants to drill in a forbidden part of the

desert where the Shariman was kept in ancient times and was believed lost through the demon Egokhan.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Palace courtyard, gardens, council hall, terrace. Objectives: greet Ben, catch the Shadow, find both parents, overhear the council, view the forbidden zone from the terrace.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Movement, interaction, dialogue tone, Shadow call (taught as a chase through the gardens). No combat.

**Fail states [G]:** None. This is the safe chapter that teaches the player what is at stake.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Arrival cinematic, family reunion, the Rashid council scene. Unlocks Shadow Scout.

### Chapter 2: The Tail of the Scorpion

**Story beats [V]:** In Oxford, Tilda finds the legend of the Shariman and concludes that the changes in the scorpion constellation signal the fulfilment of a foretold destiny. In the desert Farhat trains under Wings. Egokhan allies with Rashid and they plan to draw Farhat into a labyrinth where his parents will be held prisoner. Dolunai urges her son to begin a long journey to reassemble the Shariman and save the world from Egokhan. (Order of these scenes within the episode is confirmed in the episode check.)

**Level and objectives [G]:** Three parts: (1) a short playable Oxford library scene as Tilda (tutorial for decoding); (2) desert training arena with four lessons; (3) the first labyrinth.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Lessons: guard and parry, dodge, heavy strike, light channelling. Alchemy tutorial: gather desert plants, brew a first Heal. Labyrinth enemies: Egokhan's smoke wraiths and illusions of the parents.

**Fail states [G]:** Health failure; dead ends in the labyrinth cost time; failing a lesson repeats it.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Wings's introduction; Dolunai's request; the villains' plan cutaway. Unlocks the sword combo, light meter and Heal potion.

### Chapter 3: A Genius Named Tilda

**Story beats [V]:** Wings gives Farhat a map drawn ages ago by the sage Zenem. Farhat cannot read it, so with Wings and Ben he goes to England to ask old Professor Muff at the college where he once studied. In place of the professor he finds the young Tilda, who

translates the cryptic map at once and tells him the place and exact moment to retrieve the first fragment.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Travel sequence, Oxford campus hub (library, courtyard, lab), a map-decoding set piece.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Decode system (first full use). Light social interaction. No combat.

**Fail states [G]:** Puzzle failure only (resets).

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Meeting Tilda. Unlocks the Decode system and the world map with the first target. Whether Tilda joins the party in this chapter or later is confirmed in the episode check [I].

### Chapter 4: The Light of the Shariman [I]

**Story beats [I]:** First fragment retrieval at the place and moment Tilda calculated. Confirm actual events, antagonists and location.

**Level and objectives [G]:** A timed retrieval: reach the site before the exact moment, then activate the fragment during the alignment.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Timed traversal, light channelling, first fragment interaction.

**Fail states [G]:** Objective failure if the moment is missed (reset the approach).

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** First fragment cutscene. Unlocks light-meter tier one.

### Chapter 5: In the Great Darkness

**Story beats [V]:** Egokhan kidnaps the Shadow and takes it to the buried town of Sepolta, sealing it in the Cell of Great Darkness to dissolve slowly. Farhat must descend through quicksand to retrieve it, but Egokhan blocks the way back. On the surface, Tilda, Wings and Ben use a drilling device they borrowed from Rashid to clear the path Egokhan created.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Parallel play: Farhat below (quicksand descent, Sepolta streets, the Cell) and a surface segment where the player controls Tilda, Wings and Ben to drill the blocked path.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Shadow locked. Quicksand physics, rope-anchor puzzles, light-only visibility in Sepolta, drill gadget. Mini-boss: Egokhan's blocking wall of stone guardians.

**Fail states [G]:** Dissolve meter reaches zero (objective failure); quicksand (reset to last ledge); health failure.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** The kidnapping; the rescue reunion; the surface drilling. Restores the Shadow with Swap unlocked.

### Chapter 6: Ambush in the Desert [I]

**Story beats [I]:** Ambush in the desert. Confirm antagonists, trigger and outcome.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Open desert arena. Survive waves, then escape to the next waypoint.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Wave combat with Egokhan constructs and Petroglobe drones; a possible second fragment retrieval [I].

**Fail states [G]:** Health failure; objective failure if the wagon, camp or companion is lost.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Ambush opening; escape. Unlocks Boost potion recipe.

### Chapter 7: A Trip in a Balloon

**Story beats [V]:** Making their getaway, Farhat and his friends board a hot air balloon and flee toward Cappadocia. Egokhan unleashes a fleet of aggressive, monstrous balloons against them. Meanwhile Rashid tries to take the magic crystal with a machine that generates an artificial shadow, but Farhat's Shadow proves more capable than Rashid's.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Aerial chase over rock towers and valleys, then a landing set piece. Objectives: keep the balloon alive, steer through gates, beat the monster balloons, win the shadow contest.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Balloon steering and burner control, fire-and-dodge combat from the basket, Tilda working the instruments. **Shadow contest:** a short duel where the player controls the Shadow in a fast-reaction chase against Rashid's artificial shadow. Boss: the lead monster balloon.

**Fail states [G]:** Chase failure (balloon damaged, restart from last gate); objective failure if the crystal is taken in the shadow contest.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Escape launch, Cappadocia reveal, the shadow contest. Unlocks the balloon as a hub vehicle and Shadow Shape.

### Chapter 8: Watch Out for Miss Keyhole

**Story beats [V]:** Oxford University is in a grave scientific crisis because Tilda has been away so long. Miss Keyhole, Tilda's dreadful tutor, is sent to persuade her to return. She reaches the friends in a dusty desert village and, through a mistake, unwittingly takes possession of the two Shariman crystals Farhat had retrieved. Wings's soft side saves the day and the crystals return to Farhat. Miss Keyhole never realises the wild events that happened around her.

**Level and objectives [G]:** A dusty desert village hub with houses, a market and a well. Objective: recover the two crystals without Miss Keyhole noticing anything supernatural.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** *Cover-up system:* an awareness meter rises with noise, visible magic and combat in her line of sight; the player uses distractions, the Shadow in disguise forms, and Wings's calm negotiation. Light comic combat only out of her view.

**Fail states [G]:** Awareness meter full: she takes Tilda aside and the section resets.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Keyhole's arrival, the mistaken pickup, Wings's softening. Unlocks the Keyhole running gag (cameo in later chapters) and a cosmetic for Tilda.

### Chapter 11: Snarchkoff Against Snarchkoff

**Story beats [V]:** Rashid kidnaps Tilda and brings her to Petroglobe Palace in the Emirates. To rescue her, Farhat uses one of Wings's powerful potions to turn into Rashid. It works perfectly, but Egokhan destroys the antidote. Farhat is imprisoned in Rashid's form and takes on Rashid's own wicked personality. Tilda and the others do all they can, tracking him into the depths of Egokhan's mazes. In the end, his mother Dolunai's sweet voice revives his true soul and breaks the spell.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Four parts: (1) infiltrate Petroglobe Palace disguised as Rashid; (2) play Farhat-as-Rashid in a cell and maze; (3) play Tilda, Ben and Wings tracking him; (4) the voice-of-Dolunai finale.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** *Disguise infiltration:* bluff guards through dialogue and posture, keep suspicion low. *Identity loss:* in part 2 the HUD gradually distorts and the dialogue options turn villainous; this is a feeling, not a branch, because the story outcome is fixed. *Finale:* a memory-echo sequence in which the player collects fragments of Farhat's own voice, family and friends to rebuild his true self. Boss: Egokhan's maze guardian.

**Fail states [G]:** Suspicion meter full (reset infiltration); identity meter full in part 2 forces a loop back to the cell; health failure.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Tilda's capture, the transformation, the destroyed antidote, Dolunai's voice. Unlocks the Transform potion recipe.

### Chapter 13: Spider Web

**Story beats [V]:** Farhat and his companions reach Mexico on the trail of the fourth fragment. An outside force makes Wings's herbs unusable, and with them his magic. The friends fall into one of Rashid's traps, in which he disguises himself as one of Ben's faithful cousins.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Mexican jungle approach, a camp, then a web-filled dungeon. Objectives: cross the jungle without alchemy, spot the fake cousin, escape the web trap.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Alchemy locked (potions fail, crafting disabled). Web traversal (cut strands, swing, burn). *Spot the cousin:* the player questions a cousin using what Ben's cousin network has taught them; the disguised Rashid has a tell. Boss: giant spider.

**Fail states [G]:** Health failure; trapped in webs if too slow; picking the wrong cousin triggers the trap.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Arrival in Mexico, the herb failure, the cousin reveal. Restores alchemy at chapter end.

### Chapter 14: The Valley of the Mayas

**Story beats [V]:** The Mayans of a lost village welcome Farhat's arrival as predestined, and their queen Shulena is fascinated by her guests, especially after Farhat and the others win a hard ball game against local champions. Rashid, dressed as a witch doctor, tricks Farhat into a pool where Egokhan infects him with the Red Death. Tilda's science and Shulena's secret jungle herbs together make the antidote that saves him. Farhat then climbs an ancient pyramid to retrieve the fourth fragment, but the pyramid has Egokhan's features and swallows him.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Village hub, ball court, a sacred pool, herb gardens, pyramid ascent. Objectives: earn the village's trust through the ball game, avoid the pool trap, craft the antidote, climb the pyramid.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** *Ball game:* a team mini-game with timing passes and tackles. *Antidote crafting:* two-station puzzle, Tilda (analysis) and Shulena (herbs) in a timer. *Pyramid climb:* traversal with trap platforms. Boss: the pyramid's face.

**Fail states [G]:** Ball game lost (retry); antidote timer runs out (Red Death overcomes, restart crafting); fall from the pyramid.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Village welcome, Shulena meeting, the disguised witch doctor, the pyramid swallowing Farhat. Unlocks Shulena as a temporary party member.

### Chapter 15: In the Belly of Egokhan

**Story beats [V]:** Inside the Egokhan-shaped pyramid, Farhat is hounded by the demon and swept through the primordial elements: ice, wind, water and fire. A rivalry grows between Tilda and Shulena, both attracted to Farhat. In the end Tilda finds the key, and everyone, Shulena included, contributes to saving the chosen one. The fourth fragment is retrieved by the end of the Mexican arc [I].

**Level and objectives [G]:** Four elemental chambers in sequence, then a combined rescue. Objectives: survive each element, find the key, reach the fragment.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** One mechanic per element: ice (sliding and traction), wind (gliding and gusts), water (swimming and currents), fire (heat vents and timing). Rescue segment: the player controls Tilda and Shulena at separate stations to feed solutions to Farhat. Boss: Egokhan in pursuit, a chase, not a fight.

**Fail states [G]:** Health failure; element hazards (freezing, drowning, burning) reset to the chamber start.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** The pyramid swallowing scene, the Tilda and Shulena exchange, the fragment cutscene. Unlocks light-meter tier two and the fourth fragment.

### Chapter 17: The Call of Queen Dolunai

**Story beats [V]:** Egokhan pretends to be ill, giving Dolunai the chance to escape the vortex that holds her. As Farhat and his companions near England for the fifth appointment with the crystal, he receives a telepathic call from his mother. Against the

advice of Wings and Tilda he turns back to help her, and, wanting to use Egokhan's apparent weakness to free his father too, falls into the demon's trap. While Wings and the others fight Rashid's giant robot, Farhat finds his way out of the labyrinth thanks to the sacrifice of his parents, who remain in the demon's clutches. To free them for good he must assemble all seven fragments.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Mid-flight decision scene, a labyrinth and vortex, and a parallel robot battle on the surface. Objectives: decide to turn back, cross the labyrinth, escape, rejoin the group.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** *The impulse trap:* the player is offered the cautious option (keep going) and the impulsive one (turn back). The story proceeds as in the show either way, but choosing the impulsive route costs a resource and locks the Shadow for the labyrinth (section 7). Parallel play: control Wings and Tilda against the giant robot while Farhat navigates the labyrinth. Boss: Rashid's giant robot (multi-phase).

**Fail states [G]:** Impulse trap penalty; health failure; labyrinth timer if the vortex closes.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** The telepathic call, the parents' sacrifice. Locks in the player-facing goal of seven fragments.

### Chapter 18: Trap of Smoke

**Story beats [V]:** In England, Tilda is reunited with her beloved father Nigel. The friends continue to Stonehenge to retrieve the fifth fragment, but Rashid chases them across a foggy moor with a mechanical bulldog and Egokhan turns the stones into a hellish trap. They fight on with the help of Nigel and a Miss Keyhole nobody recognises. Farhat retrieves the fragment at the last moment and saves Stonehenge from a diabolical spell.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Fog moor chase, then the stone circle. Objectives: reach Stonehenge, survive the moor, solve the circle, take the fragment.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Fog visibility chase against the mechanical bulldog (boss). Stone-circle puzzle: align stones with light, timed against the trap. Nigel helps with tools, and the disguised Miss Keyhole is an optional ally to spot.

**Fail states [G]:** Caught by the bulldog (restart chase section); puzzle timer expires; health failure.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Tilda and Nigel's reunion, Miss Keyhole's cameo. Fifth fragment; light-meter tier three.

### Chapter 19: Homeless

**Story beats [V]:** Farhat and friends dodge one of Rashid's scorching attacks. Tilda goes back to Oxford, wins back the trust of Miss Keyhole and the other professors, and with their help gets a new supercomputer. Ben reunites all his scattered cousins, and with them builds a new, more modern foldable tent. His Japanese cousin Ben Saburo gives him a deadly frying-pan katana. Farhat goes with Wings to collect the primitive alchemical plant on which all potions are based. It grows only in the crater of a dangerous volcano, which is exactly where Egokhan will strike.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Hub chapter with three threads: Oxford lab (Tilda), cousin gathering (Ben), volcano climb (Farhat and Wings).

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Hub management (upgrade the supercomputer and tent), the volcano climb with lava hazards, the gathering of the base plant, a boss encounter with Egokhan's lava creature. Side activity: Ben Saburo's frying-pan katana unlock.

**Fail states [G]:** Lava and heat hazards (reset to last ledge); health failure; objective failure if the plant is destroyed.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Tilda at Oxford, the cousins' reunion, the katana gift. Unlocks the base plant (all potions at tier two), the supercomputer (new Decode tier) and the foldable tent (new camp).

### Chapter 20: Ben's Betrayal

**Story beats [V/I]:** Egokhan and Rashid trap Ben's cousins over an abyss of hellfire. Egokhan appears privately and offers a trade: the Shariman fragments for the cousins' lives. Ben steals the crystals, even though they are fakes, from Farhat in order to save his cousins. Everything looks like a disaster, with the crystal and the cousins lost, but Farhat, who can smell when something is wrong, devises a countermeasure. (Full resolution confirmed in the episode check.)

**Level and objectives [G]:** Secret-meeting scene, the theft, the chase after Ben, the hellfire abyss rescue.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** *Emotional decision:* the player chooses how to confront Ben (anger, kindness, silence), a tone choice only. Chase and rescue with a time-limited cousin-rescue segment. Boss: Egokhan's trap apparatus.

**Fail states [G]:** Objective failure if cousins fall (reset segment); health failure.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Ben's confession, the fake-crystal reveal. Restores trust with a cousins bonus.

### Chapter 21: The Duellers

**Story beats [V]:** The friends are on their way to retrieve the sixth fragment. Rashid and Egokhan team up to stop them, and Egokhan sends a robotic horseman to challenge Farhat to a duel. Tilda unmasks the villains' plan and rescues Farhat from a deadly trap, urging him to keep going for the sixth fragment.

**Level and objectives [G]:** A road to the fragment, a duel arena with a hidden trap, the fragment site.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** *Mounted duel:* a lance-and-sword horseback fight against the robotic horseman. Tilda's scanner reveals the trap's weak point in the mid-fight, which the player exploits. Boss: the robotic horseman (three phases).

**Fail states [G]:** Health failure; falling into the trap (reset to the start of the phase).

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** The challenge, Tilda's reveal. Sixth fragment.

### Chapter 22: A Rose for Ali

**Story beats [V]:** Even an eternalised scorpion wears down with the years. Farhat and his friends journey toward Zenem's tower, pursued by the villains, and find the ancient immortality formula, which calls for a certain rose in the heart of a most arid place called Ar Rimal. The rose needs a rain shower, which seems impossible, until an apparition of Dolunai reminds Farhat that the desert rose is actually a stone. With a grand strategy he brings rain, by way of Egokhan, and saves Wings. Meanwhile Ben hits his head during a clash.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Tower ascent, an Ar Rimal desert trek, a rain puzzle.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Tower climb with Zenem's tablets. Desert trek with a Wings health meter that drains. Weather puzzle: Farhat brews and plants a rain-making strategy while using Egokhan's own storm against him. Boss: Egokhan's storm.

**Fail states [G]:** Wings's health meter reaches zero (objective failure, with a soft reset); health failure.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Dolunai's apparition, the rain, Wings restored. Unlocks the Weather potion.

### Chapter 23: Ben's Wrath

**Story beats [V]:** Ben has hit his head and lost his memory. Farhat follows him deep into twisted canyons, where Egokhan attacks and suppresses the power of the crystals Farhat carries. Ben nevertheless overturns the harm done in the clash, though he still cannot recall who he is. Farhat's Shadow supplies a comical solution to help bring his memory back.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Twisted canyons, wind, a suppression zone. Objectives: follow Ben, survive Egokhan's attack, restore Ben.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Wind-driven canyon traversal. *Power suppression:* the light meter and Shadow are disabled in the zone. Ben (amnesiac) fights alongside as an unpredictable ally. Boss: Egokhan in the canyon.

**Fail states [G]:** Health failure; objective failure if Ben is lost in the canyon.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** Ben's confusion, the Shadow's comic recovery. Restores Ben.

### Chapter 25: Nigel Trapped

**Story beats [V]:** The seven pieces of the crystal are united and the Shariman shines. Farhat prepares to face Egokhan, but the demon makes a diversion by sealing Nigel in the shaft of an English mine. Tilda finds out and urges her friends to save him. Before leaving, Farhat hides the Shariman in the desert sand. In England the friends reach Nigel in the coal mine while Egokhan tries to stop them with red-hot magma, and Wings comes to the rescue. Back in the desert, Farhat finds the crystal gone; Tilda realises a bird has stolen it. Farhat's Shadow chases the thief while the others escape Egokhan, who is driven by Rashid, in a spectacular way.

**Level and objectives [G]:** Three parts: hide the Shariman, coal mine rescue, bird chase and escape.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Hiding puzzle (choose and disguise the spot). Mine escape with magma rising (a rising hazard). *Bird chase:* the player controls the Shadow in a flight-chase after the thief while the party fights Egokhan and Rashid. Boss: magma flood.

**Fail states [G]:** Magma reaches the player (reset to a safe point); the bird escapes (retry); objective failure if Nigel is lost.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** The united Shariman, the diversion, the bird reveal. Maximum light meter form.

### Chapter 26: The Triumph of the Light

**Story beats [V]:** Farhat and his companions head for a great maze to face Egokhan one last time. After a fierce battle Farhat is the last one standing against his enemy, who has prepared a cruel final challenge. Egokhan has charmed Farhat's parents and made them guardians of the labyrinth, ready to die to block their son. Farhat will not hurt them, but Yassir and Dolunai fall lifeless. Overcome by grief, he hurls himself at Egokhan. The clash is tremendous and Farhat finally takes the Shariman from the place where Egokhan removed it seven thousand years ago. His parents come back to life, Rashid is defeated, arrested and sent to jail, and the Shariman proves to be the heart and fuel source of an ancient machine that brings life to the desert with clean, everlasting energy.

**Level and objectives [G]:** A five-stage labyrinth (each stage a callback to an earlier chapter), the parents' encounter, the final confrontation, the epilogue in the Emirates.

**Mechanics, enemies, boss [G]:** Gauntlet of callbacks (quicksand, element rooms, balloon sections shortened). *The parents encounter:* a non-lethal boss where the player must evade and use the light meter to break the spell instead of attacking. Final boss: Egokhan, multi-phase, using every element. Rashid's capture is a short chase.

**Fail states [G]:** Health failure; the player cannot defeat the parents (an attack is disabled), so any attempt to hit them resets the section; objective failure if the party falls.

**Cutscenes and unlocks [G]:** The parents fall, the revival, Rashid's arrest, the machine reveal. Ends Part One and unlocks Chapter Select and New Game Plus.
