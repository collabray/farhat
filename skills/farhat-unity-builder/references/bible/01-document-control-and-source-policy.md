## 1. Document control and source policy

This bible is the single reference for producing the game: story, characters, systems, all 52 chapters, failure rules and production notes. Every story statement carries a confidence tag so the team knows what can be locked and what needs a check against the licensed episodes.

| Tag | Meaning | How production treats it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [V] | Verified from a published episode synopsis | Lock |
| [I] | Inferred from episode titles, series-level summaries or neighbouring episodes | Confirm against the episode before locking |
| [G] | Game design invented for this adaptation (mechanics, levels, enemies, failure rules) | Free to change in production |

**Names:** Egokhan, Rashid, Wings, Tilda, Ben, Shadow. The alternate dub names (Ikaghan, Abdul Snarchkoff, Ali, Alì, Zenome, Aladdin) are listed in the glossary in section 16.

**Source confidence at the time of writing:** Season 1 has fully verified synopses for 18 of 26 episodes (episodes 4, 6, 9, 10, 12, 16 and 24 are title-only [I], and episode 20 is partly verified). Season 2 has a verified series-level arc and verified details for only a handful of episodes; most Season 2 chapters are built from titles plus that arc and are tagged [I]. **Required before content lock:** the writers watch every [I] episode with the licensed copies and correct the matching chapter. The chapter template (section 9) is built so that such a correction changes only the story-beat lines, not the level design.

**Dialogue policy:** this bible fixes story beats, scenes, objectives and fail rules. Line-by-line dialogue is written in production from the beats, in the show's tone, and is not copied from the series.
