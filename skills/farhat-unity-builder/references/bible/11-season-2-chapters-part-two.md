## 11. Season 2 chapters (Part Two)

Season 2 uses a compact three-line format because only the series-level arc and a few episodes are verified. Each chapter expands to the five-line template of section 9 once its episode is checked. The design lines are [G] and are written from the verified arc and the episode titles, so they hold up under most corrections.

### 11.1 Season 2 systems added

- **Namirash zones [G]:** areas where the dark complement of the Shariman makes controls and physics go backwards. Introduced in chapter 31 and used through the season. Light items and the Shariman grain restore normal rules.

- **Shariman grains [V/I]:** the Shariman must be pulverised to close black holes, and only a Guardian of Ashtarak may do it. The game turns this into a grain resource: each black hole needs a grain, and the finale title (*The Lacking Grain*) suggests a missing one, confirmed in the episode check.

- **Black-hole closing [G]:** a short light-and-timing set piece repeated, with variations, in Beijing, New York and later holes.

- **Ultra volatilla [V]:** Wings's upgraded flying transport, used for long travel and aerial set pieces.

- **Laylah party member [V/I]:** alchemy partner early, antagonist later.

- **Non-lethal boss rule [G]:** enslaved allies (Ben, Wings, Rashid) are freed, never defeated.

### Chapters 27 to 39

**Chapter 27: Too Light [V/I].** *Story beats:* the friends live happily in the Emirates thanks to the Shariman's energy; to understand where the crystals come from they plan a journey through the world of dreams to a place seven thousand years in the past. *Design [G]:* clean-energy city hub with new gadgets; the dream journey as a surreal level where layouts shift; no combat boss, a threshold guardian. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* puzzle failure only; unlocks the Season 2 hub, the dream-travel mechanic and Namirash lore.

**Chapter 28: The Genius [I].** *Story beats:* the Italian title means *the genius from the cold*; a Siberian genius arrives, matching Laylah's profile. *Design [G]:* snowfield approach, twin-genius co-op puzzle between Tilda and Laylah, first alchemy session with Laylah. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* puzzle failure; unlocks Laylah as a party member.

**Chapter 29: The Keepers of Ashtarak [V/I].** *Story beats:* Rashid, turned good, becomes a Guardian of Ashtarak, the requirement for pulverising the Shariman to close the black holes. *Design [G]:* mountain-temple hub, three trials that Rashid must pass with comic results, teaches the grain mechanic. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* trial failure resets that trial; unlocks the grain resource and Rashid as an ally.

**Chapter 30: A Hero's Fall [I].** *Story beats:* unconfirmed; the title points to a setback for Farhat. *Design [G]:* a chapter where Farhat's abilities are limited and companions carry key sections. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* health failure; a temporary ability lock.

**Chapter 31: Upside Down [I].** *Story beats:* the Namirash's reversing influence is established across the season [V]; this chapter's exact events are unconfirmed. *Design [G]:* first Namirash zone with reversed controls and gravity. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* corruption failure; unlocks the light item that cancels reversal.

**Chapter 32: The Cursed Keeper [I].** *Story beats:* a Guardian of Ashtarak is cursed (from the title). *Design [G]:* non-lethal boss, the cursed Guardian, freed by light rather than defeated. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* health failure; unlocks the cleansing technique.

**Chapter 33: The Temple of the Black Scorpion [V].** *Story beats:* Tilda identifies an ancient site in the Chinese deserts where the crystal is supposed to be hidden, but she is wrong. After a thousand adventures the friends reach the Olympic stadium, where a magical black hole has opened. Farhat is helpless against it and must find the Shariman to stop the darkness destroying the city. *Design [G]:* desert trek, temple trial, stadium finale in which the sword is useless against the void and only the light and grain work. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* health failure; stadium timer; unlocks black-hole closing.

**Chapter 34: Games Gone Crazy [I].** *Story beats:* unconfirmed; the title points to games or contests running out of control. *Design [G]:* a mini-game gauntlet in which the rules reverse under the Namirash. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* mini-game failure only; cosmetic unlocks.

**Chapter 35: Ben in Black [V].** *Story beats:* Ben is corrupted by darkness and becomes Ben in Black; he returns to himself as soon as the black hole in the Beijing stadium is closed. *Design [G]:* Ben as a non-lethal boss in the stadium; the player must reach the hole's core to close it. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* health failure; unlocks the Ben-in-Black cosmetic and restores Ben.

**Chapter 36: The Spirit of the Forest [I].** *Story beats:* the forest tree that holds Greta Swanson's spirit and advises Nigel [V]; this chapter's exact events are unconfirmed. *Design [G]:* forest exploration, spirit puzzles, a gentle emotional chapter for Tilda and Nigel. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* puzzle failure; unlocks Greta's spirit as an advice NPC.

**Chapter 37: The Apprentice [I].** *Story beats:* the Italian title means *the sorcerer's apprentice*; Laylah studies with Wings's potions, which later become black magic [V]. *Design [G]:* alchemy duel between Laylah and Tilda. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* puzzle failure; unlocks Laylah's black-potion cutscene.

**Chapter 38: Battle in the Big Apple [V/I].** *Story beats:* New York is the setting of a black-hole battle; the last black hole is closed before the mountain refuge in chapter 39 [V]. *Design [G]:* rooftop and street chase, black-hole closing, aerial support from the ultra volatilla. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* chase failure; unlocks the New York hub.

**Chapter 39: Tilda's Ghost [V].** *Story beats:* the friends take refuge in the mountains north of New York after closing the last black hole. Tilda tells Farhat about Laylah's evil schemes and Farhat banishes Laylah from the group. Farhat learns of Laylah's plan to unite the powers of Light and Darkness forever. *Design [G]:* camp hub, a confrontation scene with tone choice only, Laylah's departure. *Fail and unlocks [G]:* none; unlocks Laylah as antagonist.
