## 6. Core gameplay systems (all [G])

### 6.1 Controls and camera

Third-person over-the-shoulder, free camera in exploration, authored cameras for set pieces (chases, boss intros). Full gamepad and keyboard support, remappable. Core verbs: move, sprint, jump, climb ledges, dodge, light attack, heavy attack, parry, channel light, call Shadow, use item, switch companion, interact.

### 6.2 Combat

- **Weapon:** Farhat's sword. Light combo (three hits), heavy strike, dodge roll with brief invulnerability, perfect parry window that staggers armoured enemies.

- **Health:** hearts of light (five at start, upgraded by fragments and optional collectibles). Health refills at camp, from potions and on chapter start.

- **Enemy families:** (1) *Egokhan's constructs:* stone guardians, smoke wraiths, elemental spirits, monstrous balloons, the robotic horseman. (2) *Petroglobe machines:* drill drones, the mechanical bulldog, scorching-flame drones, the giant robot. (3) *Season 2 Darkness:* backward-gravity creatures that move reversed and black-hole spawns. (4) *Enslaved allies:* mind-controlled characters (Wings and Rashid under Laylah) fought with non-lethal rules: the goal is to snap them out of it, not to hurt them.

- **Tone rule:** enemies dissolve, short-circuit or fall back. No blood, no killing of named characters.

### 6.3 Light meter (Shariman energy)

A second bar that fills as Farhat parries, defeats enemies and holds fragments. Spend it on: **Flash** (stun all nearby), **Light Blade** (empowered strikes for a short time), **Shield** (blocks one heavy hit). Each recovered fragment permanently raises its capacity and unlocks a tier of ability. After all seven are united (ch. 25), the meter gets its maximum form.

### 6.4 The Shadow

Cooldown-based companion ability with four uses: **Scout** (send ahead, reveals hidden paths), **Slip** (passes through narrow gaps to trigger mechanisms), **Swap** (trade places with the Shadow), **Shape** (turns into an object to distract or to carry a small item). Story states: *Captured* (ch. 5, abilities locked and a dissolve timer runs), *Absent* (when the story removes it), *Evolved* (after the Season 2 revelation, ch. 48, unlocks a darkness-touching version).

### 6.5 Alchemy (Wings)

Gather herbs and materials in levels, craft at camp. Base families: **Heal**, **Cure** (status effects such as the Red Death), **Boost** (speed or strength), **Transform** (used in ch. 11 as the Rashid potion), **Weather** (used in ch. 22 for rain). The key plant for all potions grows only in a volcanic crater (ch. 19), which is the story reason for that chapter's gather mission.

### 6.6 Decode and gadgets (Tilda)

Tilda's chapters use a decoding mini-system: read a cryptic map or inscription, match symbols using her koala laptop, then trigger a mechanism or a location. Gadgets (drill, scanner, signal jammer) are tied to story items such as the toy drilling device borrowed from Rashid in ch. 5.

### 6.7 Party and companion rules

Farhat is always the active character. Companions follow and contribute **assist actions** (Wings strikes, Tilda scans, Ben delivers items). In designated segments the player takes control of Tilda (puzzles) or Wings (heavy combat). No permanent party loss; story absences are scripted.

### 6.8 Traversal and vehicles

On foot, plus: **hot air balloon** (ch. 7), **Wings's flying transport (the volatilla in Season 1, the ultra volatilla in Season 2)** for long-distance hub travel and aerial set pieces, quicksand ropes, zip-lines and wind currents in canyons. Each vehicle has a short on-rails tutorial the first time.

### 6.9 Collectibles and fragments

The seven Shariman fragments are story-gated and cannot be missed. Optional items: **herbs** (alchemy), **Ben's cousin postcards** (cousin network unlocks), **Zenem's tablets** (lore), **koala charms** (Tilda cosmetics). No collectible ever blocks story progress.

### 6.10 Relationship choices (non-canon-altering)

Dialogue choices set Farhat's *tone* (confident, kind, careful) and change small rewards and a few side lines. They never change the story outcomes of the show. The Tilda and Shulena rivalry and the Tilda and Laylah rivalry use this system for banter, not for branching.

### 6.11 Saves and checkpoints

Autosave at chapter start, after each fragment, before every boss and every set piece. Manual saves at camps.
