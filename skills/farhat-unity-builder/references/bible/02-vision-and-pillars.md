## 2. Vision and pillars

A third-person 3D action-adventure in which the player lives all 52 episodes of *Farhat* in order, as Prince Farhat, with Tilda, Wings, Ben and the Shadow at his side.

**Five pillars**

1. **Faithful journey:** the story, order of locations and character arcs of the series are never changed. Optional content sits beside the story, never inside it.

2. **Light against darkness, played, not told:** the light meter, the Shadow and the crystal fragments carry the central conflict in the mechanics.

3. **A party of friends:** each companion changes how a level plays, so the cast is felt, not just heard.

4. **Impulse against wisdom:** Farhat's flaw (acting before thinking) is taught through scenes where the quick choice costs something.

5. **Wonder for all ages:** bright, adventurous, family-friendly. Setbacks, never gore.

| Item | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Genre | Story-driven 3D action-adventure with puzzle, stealth, chase and boss set pieces |
| Camera | Third-person over-the-shoulder, with authored cameras for set pieces |
| Players | Single player |
| Length | 52 chapters, 20 to 35 minutes each: about 20 to 26 hours for a full playthrough |
| Audience and rating target | Ages 8 and up, with nostalgic adults. Target PEGI 7 / ESRB E10+ |
| Platforms | PC and current consoles. Engine-agnostic; all specs below assume a real-time 3D engine |
| Structure | Part One = Season 1 (chapters 1 to 26). Part Two = Season 2 (chapters 27 to 52) |
