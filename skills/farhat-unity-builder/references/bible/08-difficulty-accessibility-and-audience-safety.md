## 8. Difficulty, accessibility and audience safety

- **Difficulty:** Story (forgiving timing, reduced enemy health, auto-parry assist), Standard, Challenge (stricter parry windows, fewer checkpoints inside set pieces). Switchable at any checkpoint.

- **Accessibility:** full subtitles with speaker names, adjustable text size, colour-blind safe palettes for light and dark states, hold-instead-of-tap options, camera shake toggle, remappable controls, auto-aim and timing assists, narration of puzzle hints.

- **Audience safety:** no gore, no real-world weapons beyond the show's fantasy sword, no depiction of hellfire beyond stylised fire, demon scenes tuned for ages 8+. Rashid's oil-baron role is satirical, not political.
